How it works

From inquiry to confirmed booking.

  1. Reads the inquiry

    Groupy picks out dates, rooms, meeting space and budget, and asks the organiser only for what is missing.

  2. Sends a quote

    It checks your availability and rates, sends a priced offer and holds the rooms while the organiser decides.

  3. Negotiates

    It trades instead of giving ground, such as more flexibility on numbers for a larger deposit.

  4. Collects the deposit

    The organiser pays online and the booking is confirmed. Later changes, refunds and instalments are handled too.
